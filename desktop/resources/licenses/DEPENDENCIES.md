# Included tools

This distribution bundles yt-dlp 2026.08.19, FFmpeg/ffprobe from eugeneware/ffmpeg-static b6.1.1, Electron, and Sora/Manrope fonts. Versions and SHA-256 digests of installed engines are recorded in resources/engines/versions.json.

- yt-dlp source and release notices: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/tree/2026.08.19
- FFmpeg static binary build scripts and associated source releases: https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static/releases/tag/b6.1.1 and https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static/tree/b6.1.1
- FFmpeg corresponding version source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-6.1.1.tar.xz
- Electron: https://github.com/electron/electron/tree/v44.7.0 — license and Chromium notices included by Electron packaging.
- Sora/Manrope font licenses are included beside the fonts in the application.

FFmpeg binaries are provided under the terms of their accompanying GPL license. The included upstream build readme/license files identify the distribution. No modification to these binaries is made other than packaging and executable permissions.
